sorted for Mac.

Public beta. One line in Terminal installs it and opens it in Chrome. Everything runs on your Mac; the shoot disk is only read and nothing is uploaded, ever.

curl -fsSL https://kirtan-00.github.io/sorted/install.sh | bash

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, Enter), paste the line, press Enter. Two to five minutes the first time. The app opens by itself when it is done.

1

Next time

Paste the same line again (fast, it just opens the app), or open the sorted folder in your home folder and double-click sorted.app. Give it Full Disk Access the first time macOS asks, so it can read the SSD.

2

Open a shoot

Open folder, pick the disk or folder, then Index. Photos take about ten minutes per 4,000 on an M1; clips longer. If you were given a .photosort-index.zip, use Index, Import an index instead and skip the wait.

3

Tell us what happened

Top right, How it works, then Feedback, Save report to Desktop. Email that zip to purohit.krick@gmail.com. It holds what you clicked, searched and where it broke. No photos, no names, no paths.

Needs: an Apple silicon Mac (M1 to M4), macOS 14 or newer, about 2 GB free for the app and a further 5 to 20 GB for thumbnails of a big shoot. Intel Macs are not supported in the beta. Prefer a zip? Download it, unzip, then run Install sorted.command; macOS will ask you to allow it once under Privacy & Security.

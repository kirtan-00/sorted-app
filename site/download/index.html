sorted for Mac.

Public beta. Runs entirely on your Mac; the shoot disk is only read and nothing is uploaded, ever.

The quick way: one line

Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, Enter), paste this line, press Enter. It downloads the app into a folder called sorted in your home, installs what it needs, and opens it. Two to five minutes. macOS does not put up its "move to Bin" dialog this way.

curl -fsSL https://kirtan-00.github.io/sorted/install.sh | bash

From then on: open the sorted folder in your home and double-click sorted.app. Or paste the same line again; it is fast the second time and just opens the app.

The other way: zip

Download sorted for Mac

Zip, about 42 MB. Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or newer. The installer fetches the rest (about 1.3 GB, once). Because the zip comes through the browser, macOS will refuse the installer the first time and offer to move it to the Bin: click Done, then System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, Open Anyway, and double-click it again. That happens once; the one-line way above skips it entirely.

1

Unzip it (zip route only)

Double-click the downloaded zip. You get a folder called sorted-app-main. Keep it somewhere you will not delete, Desktop or Documents is fine; the app lives in that folder.

2

Double-click Install sorted.command

A Terminal window opens and does the work: its own Python, ffmpeg and the search model. Two to five minutes on a normal connection. It ends by opening the app.

Zip route only. The one-line install already did this step.

3

From then on, double-click sorted.app

Same folder. Your browser opens at the app. Give it Full Disk Access the first time it asks, so it can read the SSD.

4

Open a shoot

Open folder and pick the disk or folder, then Index. Photos take about ten minutes per 4,000 on an M1; clips longer. If you were given a .photosort-index.zip, use Index, Import an index instead and skip the wait.

5

Tell us what happened

Top right, How it works, then Feedback, Save report to Desktop. Email that zip to purohit.krick@gmail.com. It holds what you clicked, searched and where it broke. No photos, no names, no paths.

Needs: an Apple silicon Mac (M1 to M4), macOS 14 or newer, about 2 GB free for the app and a further 5 to 20 GB for thumbnails of a big shoot. Intel Macs are not supported in the beta.

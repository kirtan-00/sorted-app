The build

The beta build is sent to the moment it clears our own shoot disk test. One email, with the download and the three things to try first. Nothing to install before that.

While you wait, have ready: an Apple silicon Mac, a shoot disk with photos or clips on it, and about 20 GB free on the Mac for the index and thumbnails. The disk is only ever read.

What the beta does today

  1. Indexes a whole shoot disk on the Mac. Photos, RAW, 4K clips, drone footage. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  2. Search in plain words. "sunset on the beach", "ring exchange", "drone over the fort".
  3. Faces on stills. Group them, name a person, find every frame of them from one photo.
  4. Categories it finds itself, a drone filter, an out of focus filter, a scene breakdown for clips.
  5. Export to a folder, a plugged-in disk, or straight to a Google Drive folder.
  6. Reorganise the disk into sorted folders, with Undo.

# Naming, round two

Date: 2026-09-19
Builds on: naming.md (round one). Nothing in that file is edited; everything it proposed or rejected is treated as burned here.

## What changed since round one

Round one lost on form, not on meaning. "lightproof" is a compound adjective: it describes the product instead of naming it. "rushes" and "almirah" are borrowed nouns that arrive with their own baggage (Adobe Rush next door, a Pakistani fashion label on page one). The owner's verdict was "i need a better name", and his framing since then is the brief in five words: this sorts out post production.

So the target moved. Not a photos app, not a storage app. The stage after the shoot: ingest, log, cull, pull selects, first assembly, hand it to the editor. The name should sit in that room and sound like a thing people say out loud, the way they say Kodak, Vimeo, Figma, Notion, Frame.io, Runway, Zomato. Short, identical in Ahmedabad and in London, ownable, works lower-case as a wordmark and as a Mac icon label.

Five styles this time. Ten names. Two of each, three where a style earned it.

## Method, once

Every candidate went through WebSearch (US index) against photo, video, culling, MAM and Mac apps, plus one query with "India" appended. The three finalists also got a query aimed at apps.apple.com. This surfaces obvious product and app collisions. It is not a trademark search; before anything goes on a slide, run the pick through IP India (class 9 and 42), USPTO and EUIPO. Nothing below claims a domain is available. Every check is dated 2026-09-19.

Rules applied while screening, so you know why some obvious words are missing: burned words and their near variants are out (proof, roll, dark, light-, sort-, select-, string-, wrangl-, rush, daily, plus "photosort" and "clapper"); an existing Mac or iOS photo, video or footage app with the same name is dead, not defendable; a well-known search product owning a "find" word is dead for ownability; two-morpheme English compounds are out, that is the lightproof mistake; common Indian first names are out; anything with an aspirate or retroflex a non-Indian would trip on is out; British-only crew slang that needs a US footnote is out.

## The ten

### A. Coined words

#### 1. kadro

Angle: "frame", borrowed from half of Europe and given an ending. Cadre in French, kadr in Russian and Polish, kadro in Turkish where it also means the lineup, the squad you pick. Every frame, in the lineup. Hard K in, open O out: Kodak DNA without copying anything.

Pronunciation: KAD-roh, stress on the first syllable. Same in Gujarati-accented English and in American. Nothing rude in Hindi or Gujarati.

Tagline: Every frame, sorted before the edit.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): nothing in photo, video, culling or MAM.
- Kadro Solutions (US ecommerce consultancy, holds kadro.com): https://kadro.com/ecommerce/
- gokadro.com (strategy consulting): https://gokadro.com/
- Turkish football lineup apps use the word as a word: Kadro Kur https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/kadro-kur/id6759911665, Kadro Yap https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kadro-yap/id1030323756, Kadro Olustur https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kadro-olustur/id6746195162
- India query: a village in Jashpur, Chhattisgarh, and the same US consultancy. Nothing else.
Verdict: clean in the lane. kadro.com is gone, and App Store search in Turkey will show football apps around it.

Wordmark: lower-case in a tight geometric grotesk (Söhne or Neue Haas Grotesk), the K with a straight leg; the O can carry the icon as a frame-corner mark.

#### 2. shotka

Angle: "shot" plus the Slavic diminutive -ka, the ending that gives you vodka and babushka. The little shot. It names the unit every crew member counts in, and it sounds like a thing you buy, not a thing you describe.

Pronunciation: SHOT-ka. Identical everywhere. The only echo is the drinks one, and that is deliberate: it sounds like a brand because it sounds like a bottle.

Tagline: Find the shot. Skip the scrub.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no software of any kind.
- Shotka is a Lithuanian vodka (Vilniaus Degtine), sold through shotka.com and Alibaba wholesale: http://www.shotka.com/en/ and https://www.alibaba.com/showroom/shotka-vodka.html
- Nearby but different words: ShotKam, a gun-mounted camera with a companion app (iOS, Mac-listed but not verified) https://apps.apple.com/us/app/shotkam-app/id1592489275, Shotstack (cloud video API) https://shotstack.io/, ShotGrid (Autodesk), Shottr and Shotty (Mac screenshot tools).
- India query: the vodka, and the Bengali film "Shotru" as a near miss. Nothing else.
Verdict: clean for software; class 33 (spirits) owns the word. A Google for the bare word shows a bottle first, which is the footnote risk.

Wordmark: lower-case, slightly condensed, the k with a long arm; it wants a slab or a chunky sans, not a thin one.

### B. Real short English words, bent toward find, sort, keep, see

#### 3. ferret

Angle: the verb, not the animal. To ferret something out is to find it by going down every hole until it turns up, which is what the person with 3,700 frames and a deadline is doing. Two syllables, friendly, and the animal draws its own icon.

Pronunciation: FERR-it. Same everywhere.

Tagline: Ferrets out the shot you need.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no culling, MAM or footage app called Ferret. Crowded with unrelated ferrets.
- Ferret Tools, companion app for an inspection camera, does take photos and video (iOS, also listed on the IN store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ferret-tools/id1275320105
- Ferret, NOAA's data visualisation package, has a Mac build: https://ferret.pmel.noaa.gov/Ferret/downloads/ferret-mac-os-x-downloads
- Ferretly (social media screening): https://app.ferretly.com/
- India query: the same Ferret Tools, FerretTrack (pet care), Ferret WiFi. Nothing in the lane.
Verdict: usable. The inspection-camera app is the one a trademark examiner would raise.

Wordmark: lower-case in a warm humanist sans (Inter Tight or GT America), double r set tight; the icon is the animal's head in profile, one colour.

#### 4. trawl

Angle: the editor's own verb. "I trawled through the rushes" is a sentence every AE has said. One syllable, and it carries the fishing-boat image from the product's own demo query. Veraval trawlers off the Gujarat coast, if you want it local.

Pronunciation: TRAWL, rhymes with crawl. Same in Indian and British and American English.

Tagline: Trawl the rushes. Land the selects.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no photo or video management app called Trawl.
- Trawl, Fishing Companion (iOS, identifies your catch from a photo): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trawl-fishing-companion/id6748068820
- TRAWL, self-hosted web scraping engine: https://apps.truenas.com/catalog/trawl_community/
- Trawl, lead-generation tool: https://trawln.com/
- India query: the same fishing app and scraper. Nothing local.
- apps.apple.com query: only the fishing app and fishing-industry tools (Scot-Trawl, Trap Tracker). No plain "Trawl" on the Mac store.
Verdict: usable. The fishing app may hold the plain name on iOS, which matters only if an iPhone companion ever ships.

Wordmark: lower-case, wide-set, in a grotesk with a flat-topped t; the word already looks like a label on a crate.

### C. Indian-language roots that still sound like a product

#### 5. byora

Angle: Hindi for details, the breakdown, the full account of a thing. What the product hands you is the byora of the shoot: every scene, every face, every category, laid out. It reads as a coined word to a non-Indian ear and a plain word to an Indian one, the Zomato trick.

Pronunciation: BYO-ra, two syllables, the b and y run together. A non-Indian may say bee-OR-a on first sight; it corrects itself the first time they hear it. No aspirates, nothing rude.

Tagline: The whole shoot, broken down tonight.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): nothing in software of any kind.
- Byora Homes, an Ahmedabad home-decor brand, chose the word for the same reason (their site says it means "details"): https://byorahomes.com/
- Meri Fasal Mera Byora, a Haryana government farm-registration app, removed from Google Play in 2023: https://www.appbrain.com/app/meri-fasal-mera-byora/com.mfmb.hry
- India query: Byora Homes on Myntra, IndiaMART and Brown Living. Nothing else.
Verdict: clean for class 9 and 42. The decor brand is in Kirtan's own city, which is a conversation, not a block.

Wordmark: lower-case in a serif with a real italic (Tiempos or GT Sectra), because the word is soft and the y wants a tail.

#### 6. chalni

Angle: the sieve. Hindi chhalni, Gujarati chaalni, the kitchen thing that keeps what matters and lets the rest fall through. That is the product's whole job, said in one household word. Search Amazon India for the word and you get the object, which is the right kind of collision.

Pronunciation: CHUL-nee. The app spelling drops the aspirate, so a non-Indian says chal-nee and is right. Two syllables, same stress everywhere.

Tagline: Sifts 3,700 frames down to forty.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no app called Chalni or Chhalni on either store.
- Nearest names are different words: Chali (expense tracker) https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nextappcode.chali, Challani (unrelated) https://apps.apple.com/in/app/challani/id6476611614
- India query: kitchen sieves on Amazon.in, the Chalo bus app as a near miss. Nothing in the lane.
Verdict: clean. Spelling drift (chalni, chhalni, chaalni) is the only management job.

Wordmark: lower-case in a monospace or a rounded grotesk; the icon is a sieve seen from above, a disc with a dot grid.

### D. Film and photo craft words

#### 7. gobo

Angle: the gaffer's cut-out. A gobo goes in front of a light and shapes it into exactly what the shot needs: a window, a leaf pattern, a slit. This app is the gobo for a shoot disk: 3,700 frames go in, the forty the edit needs come out in shape. Every DOP, gaffer and editor knows the word; nobody outside film does, and that is fine, because it sounds like a product before it means anything.

Pronunciation: GO-bo. Two open syllables, no way to get it wrong in any accent.

Tagline: Plug in the disk. Post, sorted.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no photo, video, culling, MAM or footage app called Gobo. The neighbours are lighting-design tools, which is the right neighbourhood and a different job.
- Omino Gobo, Mac app that turns a projector into a stage light: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/omino-gobo/id800905067
- iGobo, Apollo's gobo pattern catalogue (Mac-compatible): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/igobo/id397032703
- Rosco's free gobo viewer for iPhone: https://www.rosco.com/promotions/goboapp/
- Gobo Box Office, ticketing (Mac): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gobo-box-office/id6470237867
- Gobo: Order and Sell Local, a Nicaraguan super-app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gobo-order-sell-local/id6504262676
- India query: nothing but Goibibo as a fuzzy match.
- apps.apple.com query: every hit is "Gobo something" or "something Gobo". No plain "Gobo" on the Mac store surfaced.
Verdict: cleanest of the ten for the job. Theatre-lighting people will expect a pattern library for one second; the tagline fixes that.

Wordmark: lower-case, four letters, two of them circles; set in a geometric sans (Futura-adjacent, or ABC Diatype) with the two o's slightly larger than the x-height would give them. The icon is the object itself: a disc with a shape cut out, light coming through.

#### 8. fixer

Angle: two craft meanings, both right. In the darkroom, fixer is the bath that makes the image permanent. On a foreign shoot, the fixer is the local who finds anything, anyone, anywhere, by tonight. "The fixer sorts it out" is already a sentence. It fits the owner's line without a footnote.

Pronunciation: FIK-sur. Same everywhere.

Tagline: The fixer who sorts out post.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no exact "Fixer" in the lane, but the photo neighbourhood is busy.
- Photo Fixer: AI Image Enhancer (Mac-capable, App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/photo-fixer-ai-image-enhancer/id6648758199
- Photo Fixer: Photo Enhancer (second app, same idea): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/photo-fixer-photo-enhancer/id6447225561
- EaseUS Fixo, video and photo file repair for Mac: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/easeus-fixo-ai-file-repair/id6473367605?mt=12
- India query: Fixer, a household-repairs bidding app https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.fixerapp.fixer&hl=en_IN, and two "film fixer in India" line-production sites, which confirm the word is alive in the trade: https://www.filmfixerinindia.com/
Verdict: usable with eyes open. "Photo Fixer" means a search for "fixer photo app" will not show you first.

Wordmark: lower-case in a compact grotesk, the x as the mark; reads like a crew credit.

### E. Post-production words

#### 9. assembly

Angle: the first cut. In every edit room, the assembly is what the assistant editor hands the editor: everything usable, in order, before anyone makes a creative decision. That is exactly the product's output. Three syllables, but it is the most post-native word on this list.

Pronunciation: uh-SEM-blee. Same everywhere.

Tagline: From shoot disk to first assembly.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no footage or culling app, but the exact name already sits on the Mac App Store and a creative app uses it too.
- Assembly, an assembly-language learning app, exact name, Mac App Store: https://apps.apple.com/de/app/assembly/id639923995?mt=12
- Vector and SVG Maker, Assembly, Pixite's design app: https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/vector-svg-maker-assembly/id1024210402
- Assembly, HR recognition platform (also on the IN store): https://apps.apple.com/in/app/assembly/id6547871495
- assembly.com, an AI app builder, holds the domain: https://assembly.com/
- India query: Assembly Software Solutions India, an offshore dev shop: https://assembly-infotech.solutions/
Verdict: the exact-name Mac App Store listing is a hard block for the plain name. It survives only as "Assembly by CrayWingz" or a similar qualified listing, which weakens it.

Wordmark: lower-case in a wide grotesk, tracked out; it is the only name here that wants to look like a title card.

#### 10. pulls

Angle: what the AE does with the good takes: pulls them. "Send me the pulls" is a real sentence in a post house. One syllable, hard consonant, and it names the product's export exactly.

Pronunciation: PULLZ. Same everywhere.

Tagline: Pull the selects, hand off post.

Conflict check (2026-09-19): no app called Pulls anywhere.
- Pull App, a Mumbai OTT streaming service and production company, is the one uncomfortable neighbour: https://www.pullapp.in/
- India query: news about apps being pulled from stores, and the same Pull App. Nothing else.
Verdict: clean on the stores, but a plural verb is a strange thing to say out loud as a product ("open it in pulls"), and Pull App sits in Kirtan's own industry.

Wordmark: lower-case, the double l as two vertical strokes that can become the icon.

## Ranked

### 1. gobo

Round one failed because "lightproof" explains itself. Gobo does the opposite: it sounds like a product first and means something second, and the meaning, when a crew member gets it, is the product's job in one object. A gobo shapes light into exactly what the shot needs; this shapes a shoot disk into exactly what the edit needs. Two open syllables that cannot be mispronounced in Ahmedabad, London or Los Angeles. Four letters, two of them round, which is a wordmark and an icon already. It came back clean across photo, video, culling, MAM and Mac apps; the only neighbours are stage-lighting catalogues, which is the right street and a different shop. It sits in the craft world without being a compound, a borrowed noun with baggage, or a word a big brand already owns. It is the name that travels: a Danish DOP and a Surat wedding filmmaker say it the same way and both smile.

### 2. kadro

The strongest coined word, because it is not quite coined: it means frame in French, Russian, Polish and Turkish, and lineup in Turkish, so it arrives with a story for a European buyer and reads as a clean invention to everyone else. Hard K, open O, five letters, stress on the first syllable in every accent. Every frame, in the lineup. Its collisions are all outside the lane (an American ecommerce consultancy, Turkish football-lineup apps), and it has no competitor anywhere near photo or video. The cost is kadro.com, which is taken, and the fact that it needs one sentence of story where gobo needs none.

### 3. trawl

The best of the real words. It is the editor's own verb, it carries the fishing-boat image that is literally in the product's demo query, and it claims "search" without saying search. One syllable, sayable identically, ownable as a wordmark. It ranks third rather than higher because a consumer fishing app may already hold the plain name on iOS, and because "trawl" can read as slow and indiscriminate to someone who has never used it as a verb. Ferret is its friendlier twin and would be fourth.

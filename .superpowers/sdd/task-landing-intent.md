# lightproof landing: design intent

1. Idea: the page is a darkroom. The ground is warm black lit by a safelight; content does not fade in, it develops (masks lift like a print coming up in the tray). Searching the demo is developing: matches come up, the rest stay unexposed.
2. Palette (hardcoded, no custom properties): ground #0c0a09, fibre paper #ece6da for type, muted ink #9b9287, hairline rgba(236,230,218,0.14), safelight red #ff4a3a for marks, cursor, the vows and small mono file names (5.8:1 on the ground, passes AA at every size used), deep red #b3241a for fills. No orange, no purple, no gradients as decoration; the only gradients are inside the abstract sepia tiles that stand in for photos.
3. Type: JetBrains Mono for the wordmark ("light" solid, "proof" at 70 % ink), all UI chrome in the mocks, numbers and small labels (tabular figures). Newsreader (opsz axis, weights 300 to 500, real italic) for every headline and paragraph. Headlines large and light, sentence case, no tracked caps eyebrows, no accented single word.
4. Layout: 12-column grid inside a 1360 max, 24 px gutter at phone, 64 px at desktop. Left aligned throughout. Sections separated by one hairline and space, not by boxes. Mocks are built from CSS and SVG only (app window, tile grid, timeline with segment labels, category bar, folder tree); tiles are single-hue sepia gradients with a tiled SVG grain.
5. One spend of boldness: the hero mock app that types a query and develops the matching tiles in place, with the cursor turning into a labelled "search" state over it. Everything else stays quiet.
6. Motion language: wheel-hijacked lerp smooth scroll on native scroll position (no wrapper transform, so pinning works); mask reveals on scroll (clip-path lines and images); one pinned scrubbed section for plug in, index, search, export (the only numbered sequence on the page, because it is one); magnetic CTAs; drawn underlines on text links; feature cards tilt toward the cursor and reveal one detail line; dot plus lagging ring cursor with "search", "play" and "open" labels. Reduced motion or coarse pointer: native scroll, no cursor, no tilt, simple fades only. JS off: everything visible top to bottom.
7. Sections: hero (wordmark, tagline, two CTAs, live mock); the run (the real M1 numbers as a mono ledger); plug in, index, search, export (pinned scrub); ask it in plain words (query chips plus the category bar with the less sure band); four ways in (person, scene breakdown, drone, sharpness; asymmetric grid, not three-up cards); export (folder tree, links or copies, the index bundle); the word (why lightproof, offline, no account, no upload, disk untouched); datasheet (formats, Sony card hygiene, S-Log3, free space check); early access.
8. Copy: Kirtan's voice, concrete first, second person, dry. Only the claims in the brief, numbers verbatim. No pricing, logos, testimonials, counts or dates. No em dashes, no double hyphen anywhere, no emoji.
9. Defaults I am deliberately not using: fade-slide-up on every section, three icon cards, stat tiles, purple or blue gradients, glass cards, an arrow glued to every link, all-caps labels, a middle-dot meta string, a live feTurbulence filter over the page.
10. Verify by looking: Playwright at 1440x900 and 390x844 (top, mid, bottom), four frames inside the pinned range, a JS-off shot, a reduced-motion shot, console and pageerror clean, scrollWidth at 390 never above 390.
